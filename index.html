Common questions

How fast can you start?

Usually within a week. We scope the first sprint together and get moving.

What's the minimum commitment?

One two-week sprint or one month. No long-term contracts. If it works, we keep going. Minimum engagement is $3,000/month, or pay annually and save.

Do you work in our tools?

Yes. Jira, Linear, Figma, Notion, Slack, GitHub — whatever your team already uses.

Can we start with just one role?

Absolutely. Most clients start with a single sprint or a one-month retainer and expand from there.

Do you replace our team?

No. We fill the gaps your team doesn't cover yet. We work alongside your engineers, not instead of them.

What if we eventually hire for these roles?

That's the goal. We help you move fast now and hand off cleanly when you're ready to build the team in-house.
