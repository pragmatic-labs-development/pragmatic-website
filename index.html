The real problem

Most teams don't need "more software."
They need clarity.

Startups usually don't fail because nobody can write code. They struggle because the idea is trapped in someone's head, the UX is unclear, the roadmap is fuzzy, or the story is too complicated to sell.

01

The idea is fuzzy

"Our CEO has 47 ideas, but none of them are scoped."

02

The workflow is messy

"We know it's broken, but we don't know what software to build."

03

The product feels rough

"Our app technically works, but the UX makes people squint."

04

The story is hard to explain

"We need slides that explain what we do without losing everyone."

What we do

  • Epics & user flows
  • MVP scope & priorities

  • Founder idea extraction

  • Design critique & cleanup
